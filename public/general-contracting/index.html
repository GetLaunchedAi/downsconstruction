Full-Service General Contracting for Homes & Businesses


Design • Build • Remodel


Downs Construction LLC is a Southwest Michigan general contractor specializing in additions, whole-home remodels, kitchen & bath renovations, basement finishing, exterior upgrades, and light commercial build-outs. Our team provides clear scopes, detailed estimates, and craftsmanship that lasts.



Our Proven Process

  1. Consultation & site walk: goals, budget, timeline, and feasibility
  2. Scope & estimate: materials, trades, schedule, and permit planning
  3. Build & manage: dedicated project lead, quality checks, final walkthrough
