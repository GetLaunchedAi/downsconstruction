About Downs Construction LLC

At Downs Construction LLC, nearly three decades of experience in the building trades provide the foundation for every project. With a reputation for craftsmanship, integrity, and continual improvement, the company delivers reliable construction services that stand the test of time.

Specializing in home renovations, additions, structural repairs, and specialty carpentry, Downs Construction combines proven practices with strong industry partnerships to ensure quality, precision, and cost-effectiveness. Each project is guided by clear communication, attention to detail, and a commitment to completion on time and within budget. Downs Construction LLC is dedicated to building and restoring spaces that reflect durability, functionality, and long-lasting value for clients throughout the region.

I truly love and am passionate about what I do and go after continual improvement every day.

Brian Downs Jr CEO-Founder
